First questions

Is it halal?

No deal carries interest. We do not use convertible loans — only future-equity agreements, musharakah, murabaha, ijarah and istisna. Returns are never guaranteed; profit and loss are shared by contract. A Shariah board approves every deal before capital is called.

Who can participate?

Qualified investors and business angels under Kyrgyz law, including citizens and residents of other countries. Status is verified before the fund agreement is signed. Deal terms are never published or mailed — they are disclosed in person.

Why are there no live deals or returns on this site?

The law prohibits a venture manager from publicly offering participation while raising capital. We show completed deals only. Live deals are in the participant cabinet.

How do I send money from the US and get it back?

By bank transfer to the fund account at a bank with an Islamic window, on a capital call for a specific deal. Returns go to the account the money came from. We prepare English documents for your tax filing.
